# Hypotheses

> Testable statements the project depends on. Read during methodology work.
> Each hypothesis says in advance what result would support or refute it.
> Status values: `open` · `testing` · `supported` · `refuted` · `inconclusive` · `dropped`.
> When `/result-to-claim` gives a verdict, update the status here and link the results file and the wiki claim/experiment id.
> IDs are sequential: `H-001`, `H-002`, …

## Entry format

```markdown
### H-000 · <hypothesis in one line>
- **Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Statement:** precise, falsifiable form
- **Prediction / test:** experiment and metric that decides it (e.g. "IQM return over 10 seeds, method > baseline")
- **Supported if:** …
- **Refuted if:** …
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** experiments/results/<file>.json · wiki exp:<id> / claim:<id>
- **Links:** D-###, B-###, idea:<id>
```
